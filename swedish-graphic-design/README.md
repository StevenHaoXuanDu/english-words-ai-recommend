# Swedish Graphic Design — A Study in Form

A 3-minute motion graphics film about Swedish graphic design, told almost
entirely through geometry, grids, colour and typography.

**[swedish_graphic_design.mp4](swedish_graphic_design.mp4)** · [storyboard.png](storyboard.png)
(one frame every 3 s)

| | |
|---|---|
| Duration | 180.000 s (5,400 frames) |
| Picture | 1920 × 1080, 16:9, square pixels, constant 30 fps, H.264 High, BT.709 |
| Sound | Original score, AAC 48 kHz stereo, −16 LUFS integrated, −1.5 dBFS peak |
| Medium | 2D vector animation and kinetic typography; no footage, photos or stock |

Every frame and every sound is generated by the code in this folder:
pycairo draws the vectors, numpy/scipy synthesise the music and ffmpeg
encodes the result.

## Scenes

| Time | Scene | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 00:00–00:20 | 01 The Beginning of Form | Empty sheet. A black circle appears, a line extends from its edge, a blue rectangle and a yellow square enter. A faint grid shows, everything snaps to it, the rectangle expands into a colour field, the circle sinks into a semicircle on the divider and **SWEDISH GRAPHIC DESIGN** is revealed by yellow bar wipes. |
| 00:20–00:45 | 02 The Power of Geometry | Circle meets rectangle, their overlap becoming negative space. Their edges extend into lines that divide the frame into near-golden sections. The circle becomes a square that multiplies 1 → 2 → 8 → 32 → 128 into a pattern that rasterises a circle. The pattern condenses into a black mass that a large blue circle crosses. |
| 00:45–01:10 | 03 Grid and Order | A 4-column grid is drawn. **ORDER** fills into coloured blocks that snap into cells. Blocks leave the grid. **BALANCE**, set on the grid lines, collapses letter by letter into the seven rules that make it an 8-column grid. The blocks reflow into an editorial page headed **CLARITY**, whose letters become colour bands. |
| 01:10–01:35 | 04 Color as Communication | The bands merge into large fields with contrasting circles. In an inverse split screen the circles swap sides and vanish into their own colour. Blue and yellow overprint to green. In a quiet neutral composition a small red circle holds the eye wherever it goes. Blue, yellow and red change proportions and with them the hierarchy, ending in restraint. |
| 01:35–02:00 | 05 Typography as Shape | A zoom out from inside a giant F crops letterforms at the frame edges until **FORM** appears and snaps onto red guides. Its letters break into squares and circles and reassemble as **FUNCTION**, which rebuilds itself as **SPACE** and opens up. Weight, scale and line breaks set a rhythm for all three words, which then gather into one square. |
| 02:00–02:25 | 06 Systems and Patterns | The square gets a motif and duplicates ring by ring into a full-bleed pattern whose tiles turn. The identity becomes a poster, a packaging dieline, a magazine spread and a digital interface, all on the same grid, then all four side by side. |
| 02:25–02:45 | 07 Controlled Disruption | A calm, point-symmetric composition breaks: the circle outgrows its cell, rectangles slip, grid lines drift, a heavy black line cuts across. The cut has a slope of 1:2 through the centre, so the whole grid turns onto it and every element snaps into a new, stronger arrangement. |
| 02:45–03:00 | 08 The Final Composition | The vocabulary returns and rests on one rule: blue square, black circle, yellow square, red dot and a green semicircle rising from behind the line as the opening circle sank into it. **LESS, BUT BETTER.** and **CLARITY · FUNCTION · FORM** follow. The composition holds still, then fades cleanly to black. |

## Design system

- **Grid.** 120 px margins around a live area of 8 × 4 modules of 210 px
  (4 × 2 of 420 px, 16 × 8 of 105 px). The frame centre is a grid crossing.
- **Palette.** Warm off-white `#F1ECE3`, charcoal `#1F2022`, Nordic blue
  `#3B5E7F`, forest green `#2E5341`, pale yellow `#EFD48A` and vivid red
  `#D13F2E`, used sparingly. Flat fills only, no gradients.
- **Type.** Inter Display / Inter, shaped with HarfBuzz (kerning, caps forms)
  and drawn from their outlines so letters can be masked and moved. Scene 05
  uses a constructed geometric alphabet (`sgd/letters.py`) built from quads
  and ring segments, so letters can come apart, change weight and morph.
- **Motion.** Smooth, controlled easing with clear start and end positions:
  cubic/quartic in-outs, exponential snaps, no bounce or elastic. Most
  hand-offs are match cuts on shape, position and colour.
- **Texture.** One static paper grain applied identically to every frame,
  so the image never flickers.

## Sound

The score runs at 96 BPM in 4/4. One bar is 2.5 s (75 frames), so every scene
boundary in the brief (20, 45, 70, 95, 120, 145 and 165 s) falls on a bar line.

- **Instruments.** A slow chord bed in D Dorian / F major (common tones
  sustain across chord changes), a soft sine-kick pulse, filtered hats and
  clicks, a sine bass, a mallet-like FM arpeggio and a convolution reverb.
- **Sync.** Each scene declares an `EVENTS` list (snaps, ticks, chimes, low
  accents) computed from the same timing constants as its animation, so
  accents land on the frames they belong to.
- **Dynamics.** The opening, the neutral red-dot plate and the typographic
  zoom are deliberately sparse. In the disruption the pulse stops for a
  dissonant drone and a single low hit, then resolves.

## Rendering

Requirements: Python 3.10+, `pycairo numpy scipy fonttools uharfbuzz pillow`,
ffmpeg with libx264, and the Inter / Inter Display OTF fonts (the path is set
in `sgd/typography.py`, `FONT_DIR`).

```sh
pip install pycairo numpy scipy fonttools uharfbuzz pillow
python3 render.py                          # full film, about 2.5 min on 4 cores
python3 render.py --start 95 --end 120     # one section
python3 preview.py sheet 0 20 0.5          # contact sheet into build/preview/
python3 preview.py still 17.5              # full-size still
```

`render.py` synthesises the soundtrack, renders frame ranges in parallel
workers that pipe raw frames into ffmpeg, joins the segments without
re-encoding and muxes the audio.

## Layout

```
render.py            soundtrack + parallel frame rendering + mux
preview.py           stills and contact sheets for review
sgd/core.py          format, grid, palette, tempo, easing, primitives
sgd/typography.py    HarfBuzz shaping and glyph outlines (Inter)
sgd/letters.py       constructed geometric alphabet (Scene 05)
sgd/grain.py         static paper grain
sgd/film.py          scene dispatch, final fade, event collection
sgd/audio.py         score, sound design and mastering
sgd/scenes/s0*.py    the eight scenes
```
